The Little Prince chapter structure refers to Antoine de Saint-Exupéry,
Le Petit Prince, chapters II, V–IX, XX–XXI:
https://www.ebooksgratuits.com/html/st_exupery_le_petit_prince.html

All Chinese dialogue is an original paraphrase written for this interactive
adaptation. The deterministic Blender geometry, textures, rig and animations
were created for VirtualWorld Studio; no illustration scans or vendor models
are included. Runtime assets embed their textures and need no external service.

Build: `node generators/blender/prince/build.mjs` (Blender 5.2).
Seed: 612. Blender snapshots under assets/source/showcase/prince are local
recovery artifacts; the editable Python recipe is the source of truth.
